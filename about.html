About Gosford Community Club

Creating a space where community connections thrive

Our Story

Gosford Community Club was founded with a simple vision: to create a welcoming space where people from across the Central Coast can come together to socialize, celebrate, and build lasting connections.

Our founders recognized the need for an accessible, affordable community hub that could serve residents of all ages and backgrounds. After years of planning and community consultation, we're excited to be opening our doors in mid-2025.

The club is designed to be more than just a venue – we aim to be an integral part of the Gosford community, supporting local initiatives and providing a space where friendships can form and community spirit can flourish.

Our Mission & Values

Mission: To create a vibrant, inclusive community space that enhances the quality of life for Central Coast residents through social connection, recreation, and community engagement.

Our Core Values:

  • Community First: We prioritize the needs and interests of our local community in everything we do.
  • Inclusivity: We welcome people of all backgrounds, ages, and interests.
  • Affordability: We believe that community spaces should be accessible to everyone.
  • Sustainability: We're committed to environmentally responsible practices in our operations.
  • Local Support: We actively support local businesses, artists, and community initiatives.
