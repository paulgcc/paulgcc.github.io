Your New Community Hub

Located on the beautiful Central Coast of NSW, Gosford Community Club is designed to be a welcoming space for locals to gather, socialize, and engage in various activities and events.

Whether you're looking to meet new people, enjoy our excellent facilities, or participate in community events, we have something for everyone.
