Frequently Asked Questions

When will memberships be available?

Memberships will be available when the club opens in mid-2025. We'll announce pre-opening membership specials closer to the date.

Are there any age restrictions for membership?

Members must be at least 18 years of age. Members and visitors who are the legal guardian of a minor can bring in a person under the age of 18 provided that they are a with the minor at all times inside the venue.

How do I renew my membership?

Membership renewals can only be processed in-person at the club. We'll send you a reminder when your membership is due for renewal.

Can visitors access the club without a membership?

Yes, visitors can access the club facilities. However, members enjoy discounted prices and exclusive benefits that aren't available to non-members.
