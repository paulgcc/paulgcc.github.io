Club Amenities

Discover the facilities and services available at Gosford Community Club

World-Class Facilities for Our Community

At Gosford Community Club, we're proud to offer a range of modern facilities designed to enhance your experience as a member. Whether you're looking to socialize, relax, or host an event, our amenities are designed with your comfort and enjoyment in mind.

Bistro & Dining

Our bistro offers a range of delicious meals at affordable prices. From casual lunches to evening dining, our menu features locally-sourced ingredients and caters to various dietary requirements.

Hours: Lunch 11:30am-2:30pm, Dinner 5:30pm-9:00pm

Sports Bar

Watch all your favorite sporting events on our large screens while enjoying a drink with friends. With multiple screens throughout, you'll never miss a moment of the action.

Features: Multiple screens, comfortable seating, full bar service

Function Room

Our versatile function room is perfect for celebrations, meetings, and community events. With modern audio-visual equipment and flexible catering options, we can accommodate events of various sizes.

Capacity: Up to 150 guests

Lounge Area

Relax in our comfortable lounge area, perfect for casual meetings or catching up with friends. With complimentary Wi-Fi and coffee service available, it's a great space to unwind.

Features: Comfortable seating, complimentary Wi-Fi, coffee service

Games Area

Enjoy a range of recreational activities including billiards, darts, and board games in our dedicated games area. Perfect for friendly competition and social interaction.

Games available: Billiards, darts, board games, card tables

Additional Services

  • Free Parking: Ample parking spaces available for members and guests
  • Wi-Fi: Complimentary high-speed internet throughout the club
  • Accessibility: Fully accessible facilities for people with disabilities
  • Community Noticeboard: Stay updated with local events and announcements
  • Member Rewards: Special discounts and promotions for club members, announced closer to opening
